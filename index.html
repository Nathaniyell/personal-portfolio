About Me.

I specialize in crafting responsive, accessible, and user-friendly applications, ensuring seamless and interactive user experiences. My expertise spans frontend and backend development, allowing me to create robust, end-to-end digital solutions.
My toolkit includes React, Next.js, TypeScript, JavaScript, Vue.js, and TailwindCSS for frontend development, and I leverage Node.js, Express.js, Firebase, PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, and SQLite for backend solutions. Additionally, I am well-versed in Git, Docker, Prisma, Cypress, Jest, and Postman.
I thrive in collaborative environments where innovation and problem-solving drive success. My approach is customer-centric, balancing organizational objectives with user needs to deliver solutions that create real impact.
